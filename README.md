# Sayniska Fasalka 8aad - Imtixaan MCQ (Science Grade 8 MCQ Exam)

Kani waa codsi mareeg (web app) heer sare ah oo loo dhisay si casri ah oo loogu talagalay ardayda Fasalka 8aad ee dugsiyada dhexe si ay u bartaan, u tijaabiyaan, una adkeeyaan aqoontooda maaddada **Sayniska** (Science) iyaga oo maraya imtixaanno su'aalo doorasho ah (MCQ). 

Codsigan wuxuu ku salaysan yahay dhammaan 14-ka cutub ee buugga Sayniska ee Fasalka 8aad kuwaas oo loo habeeyay **7 Weji (Phases)** si habsami leh oo ardayga u fududaynaya akhriska iyo barashada.

---

## 🌟 Tilmaamaha Muhiimka ah (Key Features)

- **7 Weji oo Habaysan (7 Structured Phases)**: Imtixaanku wuxuu u habaysan yahay 7 weji oo mid walba uu isku darayo labo cutub oo xiriira si ardaygu u baran karo cutubyada isku dhow:
  - 🧪 **Phase 1**: Cutubka 1aad (Waxbarashada caafimaadka) & Cutubka 2aad (Biyaha)
  - 🧫 **Phase 2**: Cutubka 3aad (Habdhiska taranka) & Cutubka 4aad (Dhirta)
  - 🌤️ **Phase 3**: Cutubka 5aad (Cimilo gooreed) & Cutubka 6aad (Fududaynta Hawsha)
  - 👁️ **Phase 4**: Cutubka 7aad (Ilayska) & Cutubka 8aad (Dhulka iyo Hawada Sare)
  - 🔥 **Phase 5**: Cutubka 9aad (Tamarta Kulka) & Cutubka 10aad (Deegaanka)
  - 🧲 **Phase 6**: Cutubka 11aad (Danabka iyo Birlabnimada) & Cutubka 12aad (Dhaqashada Xoolaha)
  - 🧬 **Phase 7**: Cutubka 13aad (Walxaha iyo Astaamahooda) & Cutubka 14aad (Habka Cilmibaarista)

- **Doorashooyin Firfircoon (Dynamic Distractors)**: Jawaabaha qaldan (A, B, C, D) waxaa si firfircoon looga soo xulaa isla cutubka ay su'aashu ku jirto si doorashooyinka ay u noqdaan kuwo macno leh oo la xiriira mowduuca.
- **Iskudhafka Fisher-Yates (Dynamic Shuffling)**: Su'aalaha iyo afarta doorasho waxaa lagu dhex xulaa algorithm-ka Fisher-Yates mar kasta oo uu imtixaanku bilowdo si su'aalo cusub loo helo mar kasta.
- **Kaydinta Horumarka (LocalStorage Progress)**: Haddii ardaygu ka baxo mareegta ama uu refresh gareeyo, wuxuu ka sii wadan karaa halkii uu kaga tagay isaga oo riixaya kaarka **"Sii wad Imtixaanka"**.
- **Badhanka Dib-u-dejiye (Reset Option)**: Haddii ardaygu rabo inuu buriyo horumarkiisii hore oo uu weji cusub ka bilaabo eber, wuxuu isticmaali karaa badhanka **"Ka bilow bilow (Reset)"**.
- **Cilad-bixin & Faahfaahin (Wrong Answers Display)**: Dhammaadka imtixaanka, waxaa ardayga loo soo bandhigayaa su'aalihii uu khalday, jawaabtii uu doortay, iyo jawaabta saxda ahayd si uu u barto khaladaadkiisa.
- **Naqshad Casri ah (Premium UI/UX)**: Mareegta waxaa lagu dhisay qaabka *Glassmorphism* oo leh midabyo indhaha u roon (Dark Theme), Google Font "Outfit", micro-animations, iyo la-qabsi buuxa oo loogu talagalay talefannada gacanta (Fully Responsive Mobile Design).

---

## 🛠️ Tiknoolajiyada La Isticmaalay (Tech Stack)

Codsigan wuxuu ku dhisanyahay si fudud oo aan u baahnayn wax adeege ah (serverless) ama dependency adag:
- **HTML5**: Dhismaha guud ee bogga iyo qaabaynta semantic-ga ah.
- **CSS3**: Naqshad dhalaalaysa, glassmorphic dark theme, animations, iyo responsive styling.
- **JavaScript (ES6+)**: Matoorka su'aalaha, dynamic rendering, Fisher-Yates Shuffling algorithm, iyo maareynta LocalStorage.

---

## 📂 Qaabdhismeedka Faylasha (File Structure)

- **`index.html`** - Dhismaha guud ee UI-ga iyo qaybaha kala duwan ee screens-ka (Phase selection, Quiz screen, Results screen).
- **`index.css`** - Habaynta midabada, layout-ka responsive-ka ah, iyo animations-ka.
- **`app.js`** - Sharciga shaqada ee quiz-ka, doorashada distractors-ka, kaydinta/sii-wadista imtixaanka ee LocalStorage, iyo soo bandhigista natiijooyinka.
- **`data.js`** - Keydka xogta su'aalaha iyo jawaabaha Sayniska oo u habaysan 7 weji iyo 14 cutub.
- **`favicon.png`** - Astaanta tab-ka ee browser-ka (Favicon).

---

## 🚀 Sida Loo Adeegsado (How to Run)

Maadaama uu yahay codsi ku shaqeeya static files, uma baahnid inaad wax ku rakibto kumbuyuutarkaaga:
1. Soo degso dhammaan faylasha mashruuca.
2. Labo jeer guji faylka **`index.html`** si aad ugu furto browser kasta (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).
3. Dooro Phase-ka aad rabto inaad iska tijaabiso oo bilow!
